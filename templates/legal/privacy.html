Information We Store

Account information (name, employee ID, email, mobile number, role, and employment details) is stored to support authentication, authorization, and internal accountability. An immutable audit log records who made which changes and when.

Records are retained for as long as an account is active. Deactivated accounts and archived records are soft-deleted and retained for audit purposes; permanent removal requires explicit administrator action.
