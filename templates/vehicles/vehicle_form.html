{# Trip detection: how a trip starts (movement validation) and ends (closure time). #}

Trip & Idle Detection

Ignition ON only opens a candidate trip. It becomes a trip once the GPS history shows the vehicle really moving — enough readings at the minimum speed, or the vehicle (or its odometer) getting at least the minimum distance away — so a parked vehicle with the ignition on, or a single speed spike, never creates a trip. The same speed and distance decide when the vehicle is stationary for the Idle alert.
